  1. The real logo, set as a stamped medallion instead of a generic icon.
  2. A catalogue cover in the logo's blue with gold lettering, so the company looks established before you read a word.
  3. Both phone numbers, Los Angeles and Orange County, one tap away on every screen.
  4. No made-up prices. The owner hadn't published any, so every service reads "by written estimate", the way he already works.

What went into it

Every review on the page is copied from the company's Google listing, word for word, with the customer's name and how long ago they wrote it. The job photos came from those same reviews.

The services list only what the company confirmed it does: drain cleaning and hydro-jetting, leak and shower valve repairs, water heaters and whole-home repiping. The quote form asks for the few things a plumber needs to call back.

It's hand-built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, loads fast on a phone, and is kept out of search results so it never competes with the company's real listing.
